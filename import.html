Back up your channel. Keep posting on YouTube.

RawReel keeps a copy of your videos with their titles, descriptions and thumbnails. If YouTube removes a video or your channel, it stays up here. It's free, and your files are read in your browser only.

Only videos you own. You can back up only videos you made or have the rights to post. Backing up someone else's channel or re-uploads isn't allowed and will be taken down under the rules.

Path A: Export with Google TakeoutAvailable now

Google Takeout is Google's own tool for downloading your original video files and a spreadsheet of their details.

1

Open Google Takeout

Go to takeout.google.com and sign in with the Google account that owns the channel. If your channel is a Brand Account, switch to that account first (top-right picture), or the export will come back without your videos.

2

Deselect all, then pick YouTube

Click Deselect all at the top of the list, scroll down and tick only YouTube and YouTube Music.

3

Choose videos and video metadata

Under YouTube and YouTube Music, click All YouTube data included, click Deselect all in that window, then tick videos and video metadata and click OK. Leave Multiple formats as it is; video metadata comes as CSV.

4

Create the export

Click Next step, choose Export once, .zip, and the largest file size so it splits into as few parts as possible, then Create export. Big channels can take hours or even days; Google emails you when it's ready.

5

Download and unzip the archive

Download every part and unzip them into one folder. Inside Takeout/YouTube and YouTube Music/ you'll find:

  • videos/ your video files, named after each title (long titles are cut short)
  • video metadata/videos.csv one row per video: Video ID, Video Title (Original), Video Description (Original), Privacy, Video State, Approx Duration (ms), Video Create Timestamp, Channel ID and more. Large channels get several videos…csv files; pick them all below.

Google changes folder and column names now and then, so the helper below accepts close variations, and JSON too.

6

Choose your files here

Pick your videos.csv, and optionally the video files so we can match them to titles.

Questions

What comes over?

Your video files, titles and descriptions. Thumbnails too: Takeout doesn't include them, so we copy your public YouTube thumbnail by video ID. Views, likes, comments and subscribers can't be moved, so you start fresh here.

Is my data private?

Yes. Your Takeout files are read by your browser on your device; RawReel never receives the CSV or video files. Only the titles and IDs you email us are sent, when you press Submit.

Can I back up someone else's videos?

No. Copied videos are taken down when the owner reports them, per the rules, and the removal is noted publicly.

Do I have to leave YouTube?

No. RawReel is a backup, not a replacement. Keep posting on YouTube; your channel there stays exactly as it is.
