Path A: Export with Google TakeoutAvailable now

Google Takeout is Google's own tool for downloading a copy of your data, including the original video files and a spreadsheet of your video details.

1

Open Google Takeout

Go to takeout.google.com and sign in with the Google account that owns the channel. If your channel is a Brand Account, switch to that account first (top-right picture), or the export will come back without your videos.

2

Deselect all, then pick YouTube

Click Deselect all at the top of the list, scroll down and tick only YouTube and YouTube Music.

3

Choose videos and video metadata

Under YouTube and YouTube Music, click All YouTube data included, click Deselect all in that window, then tick videos and video metadata and click OK. Leave Multiple formats as it is; video metadata comes as CSV.

4

Create the export

Click Next step, choose Export once, .zip, and the largest file size so it splits into as few parts as possible, then Create export. Big channels can take hours or even days; Google emails you when it's ready.

5

Download and unzip the archive

Download every part and unzip them into one folder. Inside Takeout/YouTube and YouTube Music/ you'll find:

  • videos/ your video files, named after each title (long titles are cut short)
  • video metadata/videos.csv one row per video: Video ID, Video Title (Original), Video Description (Original), Privacy, Video State, Approx Duration (ms), Video Create Timestamp, Channel ID and more. Large channels get several videos…csv files; pick them all below.

Google changes folder and column names now and then, so the helper below accepts close variations, and JSON too.

6

Choose your files here

Pick your videos.csv. Optionally pick the video files too, and we'll match each one to its title. Everything is read on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere until you press Submit, and then only the titles and IDs go in an email.

No file chosen yet.

For now, upload each video you ticked to your own free Internet Archive account (here's how), then paste its Archive ID in the box next to it and click Submit. We'll add them to your RawReel channel, usually within a day.

Submitting opens your email app with the subject "RawReel channel import" and each title plus its Archive ID filled in, addressed to RR.Support.Agent@gmail.com.

Questions

?

What comes over?

Your video files, titles and descriptions. Thumbnails too. Takeout doesn't include them, so we copy your public YouTube thumbnail using the video ID you send us. Views, likes, comments and subscribers stay on YouTube. Those numbers belong to YouTube's systems and can't be moved, so you'll start fresh here and can tell your audience where to find you.

?

Is my data private?

Yes. Your Takeout files are read by your own browser on your own device. RawReel never receives the CSV or your video files. Only what you choose to email us (titles, Archive IDs, YouTube IDs) is sent, and only when you press Submit.

?

Can I import someone else's videos?

No. Only import videos you made or have the rights to post. Copied videos get taken down when the owner reports them, as explained on the rules page, and the removal is noted publicly like any other.

?

Do I have to leave YouTube?

No. Importing copies your videos. Your YouTube channel stays exactly as it is.
