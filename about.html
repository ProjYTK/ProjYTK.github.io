About RawReel.

Your channel's backup. Classics that stay free. RawReel gives creators an easy way to keep a copy of their videos, and it hosts public domain and openly licensed classics anyone can watch.

1

The story

RawReel started after we watched creators lose years of work to sudden strikes, mistaken terminations and vanished channels. We wanted a place where a channel's work has a second home.

Back up your channel

2

The mission

A backup for your YouTube channel, alongside YouTube, not instead of it, and a free library of silent films, early cartoons and verified Creative Commons work. Each classic shows its year and why it's free to show. You can only back up videos you own.

The team

RawReel is a small team building in the open, testing everything as we go. The site is very rough and early, and it isn't close to finished. We're sharing it now so creators can tell us what they actually need. Questions? Email RR.Support.Agent@gmail.com.

Sponsors and ads
