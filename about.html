About RawReel.

A video site for people who are tired of rules that change overnight, strikes nobody explains, and algorithms that decide who gets seen.

?

Why we exist

Big platforms remove and bury videos without telling anyone why. We think creators deserve to know the rules up front and see exactly what happened when something goes wrong.

✓

What we promise

One short page of rules applied the same to everyone, a public reason for every removal, a feed in upload order with no hidden algorithm, and your videos always downloadable.

!

Where we are right now

We're brand new and run on a shoestring. Videos are stored on Internet Archive for now, and our goal is to run our own servers so nobody else's terms can decide what stays up.
