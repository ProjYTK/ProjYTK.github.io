Upload your video.

Videos are stored on Internet Archive, a free nonprofit library, and played here. About five minutes.

1

Make a free Internet Archive account

Sign up at archive.org. Free, only needs an email.

2

Upload your video there

Click Upload, add your file, title and description, and pick a license. Only videos you made or have the rights to.

3

Copy your video's ID

It's the last part of the link: archive.org/details/your-video-id.

4

Send it to us

We'll add it to RawReel, usually within a day.
