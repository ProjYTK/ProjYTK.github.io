Submit a video for review.

RawReel doesn't host video files. You put your video on Internet Archive first (that needs a free Archive account), then send us its ID. The team reviews every submission before it appears here, usually within a day. Backing up a whole channel? Use Back up your channel instead.

1

Get a free Internet Archive account

Sign up at archive.org/account/signup and confirm the email they send you.

2

Put your video on Internet Archive

On the Archive's add-an-item page, add your file, a title and description, and pick a license. Processing can take from a few minutes to a few hours for long videos. Only videos you made, have the rights to, or that are public domain.

3

Copy the item's ID

It's the last part of the link: archive.org/details/your-video-id. Pasting the whole link below is fine too.

4

Send it to us for review

We check the rights and the file, then add it. If it can't go up, we'll email you why.
