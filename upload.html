Upload Rules About

Upload your video.

While we're small, videos are stored on Internet Archive, a free nonprofit library, and played here. It takes about five minutes.

1

Make a free Internet Archive account

Sign up at archive.org. It's free and only needs an email.

2

Upload your video there

Click Upload on Internet Archive, add your file, title, and description, and pick a license you're comfortable with. Only upload videos you made or have the rights to.

3

Copy your video's ID

Your video's page will look like archive.org/details/your-video-id. The last part is the ID.

4

Send it to us

Fill this in and we'll add your video to RawReel, usually within a day.
