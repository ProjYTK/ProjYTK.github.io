Draft rules for discussion, not final

The rules. All of them.

If a rule isn't written here, we don't enforce it. Same rules for every creator.

1

Nothing illegal

No content that breaks US law, like child exploitation, real threats of violence, or pirated material that gets a valid legal takedown.

2

No doxxing or targeted harassment

Don't post people's private info or organize attacks on a specific person.

3

Label adult content

Graphic or adult videos are allowed if they're marked, so viewers can choose to see them.

4

No spam or scams

No fake giveaways, phishing links, or mass-uploaded junk.

5

Opinions are not a violation

Unpopular, controversial, or political views are never removed just for being controversial.

How enforcement works

✓

Every action is public

When a video is restricted or removed, the rule number and the reason are posted on the video's page and in the creator's moderation log.

✓

No shadowbans

Videos are never secretly hidden from search or feeds. If we limit something, we say so.

✓

You can appeal, and you can leave

Every action can be appealed at RR.Support.Agent@gmail.com, and creators can download all of their videos at any time.

©

Copyright strikes

Three uncontested DMCA strikes close an account.

!

Outside hosts

Videos are stored with hosts like Internet Archive for now, so their terms apply too. If a host removes something, we'll tell you.
