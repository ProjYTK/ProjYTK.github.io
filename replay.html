RawReplay: music from independent artists

RawReplay is the music side of RawReel. It's a free place where independent artists share music they made themselves.

To be honest, this is an early prototype. The releases you see right now are test placeholders, not real artist submissions. Things will change, break, and get rebuilt as we go.

    How artists get on RawReplay

    Today: the submit form is a placeholder. It doesn't send anything yet, so please don't use it to share your music.

    Submissions aren't open yet.

    When submissions open, here's how it will work:

    • You keep your rights. Your music stays yours.
    • Original music only. You must own, or have permission for, everything in your upload: the song, the recording, the lyrics, and the cover art. If you're in a band or worked with a producer, make sure they're OK with it.
    • No leaks or other people's music. That means no unreleased songs by other artists, no snippets or early album copies, and no rips, remasters, sped-up, slowed, or nightcore edits of someone else's tracks.
    • Samples need clearance, and covers stay out for now. Use only samples you have permission for, or ones from a royalty-free pack whose license allows it. Covers need the songwriter's permission, so leave them out for now.
    • Beats and AI tools: their license has to allow public sharing.
    • Every submission is reviewed by the RawReel team before it goes live.
    • Free to listen.

    All RawReel rules apply. We never remove a song for its opinion. If a rights owner sends a valid copyright notice, we take the track down and tell you why. You can send a counter-notice or appeal any strike. Full short rules.
