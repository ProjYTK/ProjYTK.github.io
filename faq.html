RawReel FAQ

What is RawReel?

RawReel is a free video site. Our motto is "Your channel's backup. Classics that stay free." It does two things:

  • Backs up your channel. We keep a copy of your own videos, so if YouTube removes a video or your channel, it stays up here. RawReel runs alongside YouTube. It isn't a replacement, so keep posting there as usual.
  • Shows free classics. Old films and cartoons that anyone is allowed to share.

To be upfront: the site is very rough and early. Expect rough edges.

How do I back up my channel today?

Right now it's a few manual steps, not one click (see https://rawreel.github.io/import.html):

  1. Use Google Takeout to export your YouTube videos and their details (a videos.csv file).
  2. On the import page, pick your videos.csv (and, if you want, your video files). They're read in your browser only. We never receive them.
  3. Put each video on your own free Internet Archive account, paste its Archive ID next to the title, and press Submit. That opens an email to us with the list, and we add the videos to your backup.

What comes over: video files, titles, descriptions and thumbnails. What doesn't: views, likes, comments and subscribers. Only back up videos you made or have the rights to.

Where are my videos stored?

While we're small, videos are stored with outside hosts: Internet Archive, plus a PeerTube server as a backup. Their terms apply too, and if a host removes something, we'll tell you. You keep ownership of your videos and can download them anytime. Please keep your own copies as well.

What gets removed, and what doesn't?

We never remove a video for its viewpoint. Unpopular, controversial or political opinions stay up.

Only these come down, under our written rules:

  1. Illegal content (for example, child exploitation, real threats of violence, or pirated material)
  2. Doxxing or targeted harassment
  3. Adult content that isn't labeled (labeled adult content is allowed behind an 18+ check)
  4. Spam or scams
  5. Anything under a valid DMCA copyright notice

Every removal is public. It shows the rule number and the reason on the video's page. You can appeal any removal at RR.Support.Agent@gmail.com. There are no shadowbans. Three uncountered copyright strikes close an account.

What does "classics" mean?

Films that are free for anyone to share:

  • US public domain films, such as silent films and cartoons from 1930 or earlier (Chaplin, Keaton and others)
  • US government works, such as NASA mission footage
  • Open movies under CC BY, which we show with the required credit

Some films play silent because the music added to later copies isn't cleared for sharing. Free classics: https://rawreel.github.io/classics.html

What is RawReplay?

RawReplay (https://rawreel.github.io/replay.html) is RawReel's music section: a free place for independent artists to share music they made themselves. It's still a prototype. The tracks shown are placeholders, and the submit form doesn't send anything yet. When it opens, it will take original music only (no covers, leaks or uncleared samples), artists keep their rights, and every submission will be reviewed.

What's planned?

These features aren't live yet. They have no dates, and we can't promise them:

  • Our own server: a RawReel PeerTube video server.
  • Direct uploads to that server.
  • Auto-sync: paste your channel link, prove it's yours with a short code in your channel description, and we copy the channel and then check for new uploads regularly. It starts with small limits, for example one synced channel per person.

Until then, use the Google Takeout steps above.

Is it free?

Yes. There are no ads right now. Any ads later will be short and always skippable after 5 seconds.
