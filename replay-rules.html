RawReplay rules

The short version: only post music you made and have the right to share. If it isn't yours, don't upload it.

  1. Own it or control it. You must own, or have permission for, everything in your upload: the song, the recording, the lyrics, and the cover art. If you're in a band or worked with a producer, make sure they're OK with it being posted.
  2. No leaks. No unreleased songs by other artists, no "snippets," and no early copies of albums, no matter where you found them.
  3. No uploads of other people's music. That includes full songs, rips, remasters, sped-up or slowed versions, and "nightcore" edits of someone else's tracks.
  4. Samples need clearance. If your track uses a piece of someone else's recording, you need permission from whoever owns it, or the sample has to come from a royalty-free pack whose license allows it. Covers need the songwriter's permission or a cover license, so leave them out for now.
  5. AI and beats. If you used a beat or AI tool, its license has to allow you to share the result publicly.
  6. Same site rules. Everything in the RawReel rules also applies here. We never remove a song for its opinion.

When someone says you used their music

  • If a rights owner sends us a valid copyright notice, we take the track down and tell you why.
  • If you believe the takedown was a mistake (you own it, or you had permission), you can send a counter-notice. If the other side doesn't take legal action within 10 to 14 business days, we may put the track back.
  • Sending a false notice, or a false counter-notice, can make you legally responsible.
  • Our copyright contact details will be posted here once they are ready.

Three strikes

Each valid copyright takedown is one strike. A strike drops off after [90] days if there are no new ones. A third strike while two are still active closes the account. You can appeal any strike.
