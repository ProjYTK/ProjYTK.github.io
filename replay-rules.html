RawReplay rules (short version)

Only post music you made and have the right to share. If it isn't yours, don't upload it.

  • Own it or control it: song, recording, lyrics and cover art. Bandmates and producers must be OK with it.
  • No leaks: no unreleased songs by other artists, snippets, or early album copies.
  • No other people's music: including rips, remasters, sped-up/slowed or nightcore edits.
  • Samples need clearance; leave covers out for now.
  • Beats and AI tools: their license must allow public sharing.
  • All RawReel rules apply. We never remove a song for its opinion.
  • Copyright notices: valid notices mean takedown; counter-notices are possible. Each valid takedown is a strike; a strike drops off after [90] days with no new ones; a third active strike closes the account. Any strike can be appealed.
